=====================================================================
 NPC COMBAT GAME - ENEMY, MINI-BOSS & BOSS DATABASE
 12 zones x 4 sub-zones | 3 enemies per sub-zone (2 in sub-zone 4)
 Sub-zone 4 = boss room: 2 normal enemies + 2 mini-bosses + 1 boss
=====================================================================

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. HOW THE NUMBERS WORK
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Base value of a sub-zone:
    B = 100 x 10^(zone - 1) x 2^(subzone - 1)

Normal enemies:
    Enemy A (light)    HP = 0.8 x B
    Enemy B (standard) HP = 1.0 x B
    Enemy C (heavy)    HP = 1.2 x B
    (In sub-zone 4 only B and C exist, plus the mini-bosses and boss)
    DMG per hit = 5% of HP
    COINS (base reward) = 20% of HP

Mini-bosses (2 per boss room, based on B of sub-zone 4):
    Mini-boss 1: HP = 5 x B  | DMG = 6% of HP | COINS = 30% of HP
    Mini-boss 2: HP = 7 x B  | DMG = 6% of HP | COINS = 30% of HP

Boss:
    HP = 25 x B | DMG = 7% of HP | COINS = 40% of HP

Notes:
- DMG is the damage of one normal hit. The player's max HP and weapon
  damage should follow the same curve (upgrades, levels, rebirth bonus),
  otherwise late zones are impossible.
- COINS are BASE rewards, before rebirth multipliers, game passes and boosts.
- Number suffixes: K = 1,000 | M = 1,000,000 | B = 1,000,000,000
                   T = 10^12 | Qa = 10^15

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. LOOT SYSTEM
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Every enemy has ONE possible loot item.

DROP CHANCE (per kill)
    Normal enemies : 2%   (identical for ALL normal enemies)
    Mini-bosses    : 4%
    Bosses         : 6%

RARITY TIERS
    1 Common | 2 Uncommon | 3 Rare | 4 Epic
    5 Legendary | 6 Mythic | 7 Divine | 8 Secret

The stronger the enemy, the rarer the loot. With b = ceil(zone / 2):
    Sub-zones 1-2 loot rarity = tier b
    Sub-zones 3-4 loot rarity = tier b + 1
    Mini-boss loot rarity     = tier b + 2
    Boss loot rarity          = tier b + 3 (max tier 8 = Secret)

Loot types: Corpse, Egg (pet egg), Weapon, Armor, Material, Trophy.
Suggestion: item stats scale with the zone, so a Rare item from the
Volcano is stronger than a Rare item from the Plains.

Rarity per zone summary:
    Zones 1-2   : Common / Uncommon  | Mini-boss Rare      | Boss Epic
    Zones 3-4   : Uncommon / Rare    | Mini-boss Epic      | Boss Legendary
    Zones 5-6   : Rare / Epic        | Mini-boss Legendary | Boss Mythic
    Zones 7-8   : Epic / Legendary   | Mini-boss Mythic    | Boss Divine
    Zones 9-10  : Legendary / Mythic | Mini-boss Divine    | Boss Secret
    Zones 11-12 : Mythic / Divine    | Mini-boss Secret    | Boss Secret

Line format:
    Name | HP | DMG | COINS | LOOT: item (type)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. DIAMONDS (premium currency)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Diamonds are a second currency, separate from COINS. They can be:
    - dropped by mini-bosses and bosses ONLY (normal enemies never drop them)
    - bought with Robux (Developer Products)

Because they can be bought, they are kept RARE and grow slowly:
the amounts do NOT multiply by 10 per zone like coins do.

DROP CHANCE (per kill, rolled separately from the loot item roll)
    Mini-boss 1 : 15%
    Mini-boss 2 : 15%
    Boss        : 40%

AMOUNT DROPPED (zone number = z)
    Mini-boss 1 : 2 + z   diamonds
    Mini-boss 2 : 3 + z   diamonds
    Boss        : 10 + 3z diamonds

    Zone | Zone name          | Mini-boss 1 | Mini-boss 2 | Boss
    -----+--------------------+-------------+-------------+------
     1   | Plains             |      3      |      4      |  13
     2   | Desert             |      4      |      5      |  16
     3   | Jungle             |      5      |      6      |  19
     4   | Tundra             |      6      |      7      |  22
     5   | Cursed Swamp       |      7      |      8      |  25
     6   | Volcano            |      8      |      9      |  28
     7   | Hell               |      9      |     10      |  31
     8   | Heaven             |     10      |     11      |  34
     9   | Realm of the Dead  |     11      |     12      |  37
    10   | Abyss              |     12      |     13      |  40
    11   | Mechanical City    |     13      |     14      |  43
    12   | The Void           |     14      |     15      |  46

Expected diamonds per full boss-room clear in zone z:
    0.15 x (2+z) + 0.15 x (3+z) + 0.40 x (10+3z)  =  4.75 + 1.5z
    (about 6 in zone 1, about 23 in zone 12)

Rebirth / luck boosts may increase the DROP CHANCE slightly, but should
never multiply the AMOUNT, so purchased diamonds keep their value.
