 PART A - PLAYER COMBAT (functional)
=====================================================================
Server-authoritative; rules from DESIGN.md section 2 and docs/weapon_system_document.txt.
 - **Starter weapon:** if a player owns no weapon (new player OR after a Rebirth wipe), grant one free
   Common `<zone>_sword` of the zone they are currently in/redoing (zone 1 for new players). This avoids soft-locks.
 - Input: PC mouse 1, mobile attack button, gamepad RT. Melee categories: HOLD to attack at the weapon's
   hits/sec. Ranged and Magic: ONE press = one shot (repeat only with the Auto-Attack pass). Request:
   `Net.request("Attack", aimDir, aimPoint)`, throttled to the weapon's rate with small tolerance.
 - Hit shapes (server, at the swing's impact moment):
   Sword: arc 120 deg, radius 6, up to 3 targets | Spear: line 10 long x 3 wide, pierces up to 4 |
   Heavy: arc 90 deg radius 6 + small ground slam AoE radius 6, target stagger 0.3 s | Dagger: arc 60 deg radius 4,
   single target (+20% crit chance on top) | Gauntlet: cone 90 deg radius 5, 3-hit combo, 3rd hit x2 damage |
   Ranged: projectile 120 studs/s, range 60, hits the first enemy | Magic: projectile or targeted AoE at the aim point
   within range 35, radius 6 (x (1 + Magic radius bonus)), hits all inside | Whip & Chain: cone 100 deg radius 12, up to 6 targets.
 - **Damage per hit** = formula in menu_systems_brief.txt A4 with `Util/Stats.compute` (shared), with NO zone factor (rule 1B).
 - Roll: key Q / button / B, 0.35 s invulnerability, 12 studs, cooldown 1.5 s (Mind reduces, floor 0.6 s).
   Block: hold mouse 2 / button / LT, reduces damage 60% (+Endurance, cap 85%), movement -50%, cannot attack.
   Server checks cooldowns and the i-frame/block windows with timestamps.
 - Feedback (client): hit sparks, short camera shake (Settings toggle), floating damage numbers (crits bigger/yellow),
   weapon swing animation per category (placeholder arm swing now), sounds (Sfx names from DESIGN.md).
 - Death: the player loses **35% of their Gold**, instant respawn at the Entrance of the current sub-zone
   (`workspace.Zones.<Zone>.<Zone>_<n>.Entrance`; hub spawn if none), toast "You lost 35% of your Gold: -N".
 - Auto-Attack pass (if owned): toggle on the HUD; melee auto-swings at the nearest enemy in range, ranged auto-fires at
   the nearest enemy in range.

`Id, Name, Zone, SubZone, Tier ("normal"|"mini"|"boss"), HP, DMG, Gold, Loot (type, rarity), Rig, Size, WalkSpeed,

Write a validation command `validateEnemies` that compares the config with enemy_database.txt (HP, DMG, Gold) and prints mismatches.

SPAWNING
 - Per sub-zone, **10 enemies alive at once**: sub-zones 1-3 = 4 / 3 / 3 of the three types; sub-zone 4 = 5 / 5 of its two
   normal types (the boss room's mini-bosses and boss live in the arena, Part C).
 - Spawn points: `workspace.Zones.<Zone>.<Zone>_<n>.Spawns`; if empty, random free positions inside `Area`.
 - **Respawn 2 seconds after an enemy dies**, at a random spawn point at least 12 studs from any player.
 - Enemies are active only while at least one player is inside that sub-zone's `Area`; otherwise they are paused/hidden
   (performance). Enemies are shared by all players in the sub-zone.

AI (server, 10 Hz tick)
 - States: Idle (small wander) -> Aggro (player within AggroRadius, default 30) -> Chase -> Telegraph -> Attack -> Recover ->
   Leash (target farther than 60 studs from its spawn: return and heal to full).
 - Melee attack: wind-up animation + red flash for the config `Telegraph` (default 0.4 s), damage applied at the impact frame
   if the player is in range/cone, cooldown default 2 s. Ranged attack (archers, bats' spit, etc.): projectile 60 studs/s, range 40, telegraph 0.5 s.
 - Flying enemies (bat, seagull, vulture, eagle) hover 4 studs above ground; fish (piranha, swordfish) hover/swim just above
   the ground or in shallow water (no swimming mechanics); spirits and the djinn float.
 - Default speeds (studs/s): quadruped 14, biped 12, bird 16, serpent 12, arachnid 15, golem 8, mini-boss 10, boss 10.
 - Movement: server-controlled with a root part (no heavy physics), ground snapping by raycast, short obstacle avoidance;
   PathfindingService only as a stuck fallback. Network ownership server.
 - Hit reaction: white flash 0.1 s, small knockback (not for mini-bosses/bosses), stagger from Heavy slam.

DAMAGE, REWARDS, LOOT
 - Track damage per player per enemy. On death the **top damage dealer** (tie: last hit) gets the Gold and the loot roll.
 - Gold = config Gold x the player's Gold multiplier (`Stats.compute`). Death effect: dissolve + coin pop; "+N" floating text.
 - Loot: normal 2%, mini-boss 4% (x LootLuck multipliers from Arcane, pets, Rebirth Shop). Item type mapping (DESIGN.md section 5):
   Egg -> Egg | Armor/Weapon -> Armor piece of the zone (random slot) | Corpse/Material/Trophy -> Head Trophy; rarity as listed in
   enemy_database.txt. Granted straight to the inventory (use the existing egg/armor systems; add `Trophies`); full storage
   auto-sells with a toast. Toast "Obtained: <rarity> <item>" with rarity color.
 - Trophy sale: the Trophy Merchant NPC (Part F) and the Inventory "Trophies" tab sell trophies for
   `25 x enemy Gold x rarityMult x (1 + Trophy Value bonus)`; "Sell All" button.
 - UI: HP bar above each enemy (name, rarity-less tier color: normal white, mini orange, boss red), shown when damaged or within 30 studs.

ADMIN TEST COMMANDS: `spawnEnemy(id)`, `killAll`, `setZone(zoneId, subZone)`, `giveWeapon`, `godMode`, `setGold`, `validateEnemies`, `simulateKills(id, n)`
(the last one runs n server-side kills and reports the observed drop frequencies).

STYLE RULES
 - Low-poly with **visible flat-shaded facets**, strong silhouettes, chunky readable shapes, bright saturated colors per zone palette (docs/zone_design_document.txt),
   simple glossy dark eyes, subtle gradient per part. Clean and consistent across all models; not noisy, no tiny details.
 - Triangle budgets: pets 200-800 | normal enemies 300-1,500 | mini-bosses 1,500-3,000 | bosses 3,000-6,000 | NPCs 1,000-2,500.
 - One material and one small texture per model (512 max) or vertex/flat colors baked to a 256 palette; emissive parts (eyes, crystals, runes) separated.
 - Sizes in studs: small 2-3.5, medium 4-5.5, large 6-8, mini-bosses 9-11, bosses 16-20 (boss roughly 3x a mini-boss).
 - Facing: define the Roblox front axis in the pilot, write it down, apply it to everything.
