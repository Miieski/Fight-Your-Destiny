=====================================================================
 FIGHT YOUR DESTINY - WEAPON SYSTEM DOCUMENT
 8 categories x 12 weapons = 96 weapons (one per category per zone)
=====================================================================

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. CORE RULES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- There are 8 weapon categories. Each category has exactly 12 weapons,
  one for each zone. So every zone has 8 weapons of its own (96 total).
- Every weapon belongs to ONE zone. A weapon's zone sets its base
  damage, so a Legendary weapon from the Void is far stronger than a
  Legendary weapon from the Plains.
- Weapons are bought in the SHOP. The weapon is chosen by the player but
  its RARITY is RANDOM at purchase.
- Damage is proportional to rarity (rarity multiplier).
- A weapon used inside its OWN zone gets a Zone Boost, also proportional
  to its rarity.
- A weapon with a rarity is a unique item in the inventory:
  { weaponId, rarity, level }. The player can own several copies of the
  same weapon with different rarities.

DAMAGE FORMULA (per hit, calculated on the server)
    Damage = ZoneBase x CategoryMult x RarityMult
             x (1 + ZoneBoost if the player is in the weapon's home zone)
             x (1 + other bonuses: rebirth, upgrades, boosts)

    ZoneBase = 10 x 10^(zone - 1)
    (zone 1 = 10, zone 2 = 100, ... zone 12 = 1 trillion)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. CATEGORIES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Category multipliers are tuned so the DPS of all categories is similar;
the difference is in HOW they play (reach, area, speed, safety).

 # | Category          | Dmg mult | Hits/sec | Range | Playstyle
---+-------------------+----------+----------+-------+------------------------------
 1 | Sword             |   1.0    |   1.0    |   6   | Balanced. Sweeping arc, hits
   |                   |          |          |       | up to 3 enemies.
 2 | Spear             |   1.2    |   0.9    |  10   | Long reach. Thrust pierces
   |                   |          |          |       | up to 4 enemies in a line.
 3 | Heavy (axe/hammer)|   2.5    |   0.4    |   6   | Slow, huge hits. Small area
   |                   |          |          |       | slam and a short stagger.
 4 | Dagger            |   0.4    |   2.5    |   4   | Very fast. +20% crit chance
   |                   |          |          |       | (crit = x2 damage).
 5 | Gauntlet (fists)  |   0.6    |   1.8    |   5   | 3-hit combo; the 3rd hit
   |                   |          |          |       | does x2 damage.
 6 | Ranged (bow/gun)  |   0.8    |   1.2    |  60   | Safe, single target
   |                   |          |          |       | projectile.
 7 | Magic (staff)     |   0.8    |   1.0    |  35   | Area damage in a small radius
   |                   |          |          |       | around the target.
 8 | Whip & Chain      |   0.9    |   1.1    |  12   | Wide frontal sweep that hits
   |                   |          |          |       | everything in the cone.

Approximate DPS per hit-rate (Common, relative to Sword = 10 in zone 1):
    Sword 10 | Spear 10.8 | Heavy 10 | Dagger 10 (+crits) | Gauntlet 10.8
    Ranged 9.6 | Magic 8 (but area) | Whip 9.9 (but wide)

Ranged and Magic have slightly lower single-target DPS because they are
safer and have range or area.

IDEAS FOR FUTURE CATEGORIES (not included yet)
    - Scythe: very wide arc, lifesteal.
    - Shield + one-handed weapon: blocks damage.
    - Throwing weapons: boomerangs, chakrams that return.
    - Instruments: buff allies or damage in a cone (good for co-op).
    - Pets/summons as an extra slot.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. ZONE BOOST (home-zone bonus)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- When the player fights inside the weapon's own zone (any of its 4
  sub-zones, including the boss room), the weapon gets a damage bonus
  equal to its Zone Boost (table in section 3).
- Example: a Legendary Volcano Hammer used in the Volcano deals +40%.
  The same hammer used in Hell gets no bonus.
- The higher the rarity, the bigger the boost, so rare weapons shine
  most in their home zone, and fighting a boss with its zone weapon is
  always a good choice.
- The UI shows a "Zone Boost active" icon on the weapon when it applies.

ZONE ELEMENT (optional flavor; works in every zone)
- Each zone gives its weapons an elemental effect. The chance to
  trigger on a hit equals the "Element proc" value of the rarity.

 Zone | Element    | Effect on proc
------+------------+--------------------------------------------------
  1   | Nature     | Entangle: slows the enemy for 2s
  2   | Sand       | Blind: enemy attacks miss more for 3s
  3   | Poison     | Damage over time for 4s
  4   | Frost      | Slows, then freezes briefly at max stacks
  5   | Curse      | Enemy takes +15% damage for 4s
  6   | Burn       | Damage over time for 4s
  7   | Hellfire   | Strong burn (bigger damage over time)
  8   | Holy       | Burst of extra damage and a small heal for you
  9   | Soul Drain | Lifesteal on the hit
 10   | Tidal      | Knockback and a short stun
 11   | Shock      | Chain lightning to 2 nearby enemies
 12   | Gravity    | Pulls nearby enemies together and a void burst

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 5. SHOP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- The shop has one tab per unlocked zone, with the 8 weapons of that
  zone. Zones that are locked show their weapons grayed out.
- Buying a weapon: the player pays the zone price in COINS and receives
  that weapon with a RANDOM rarity (odds in section 3), rolled on the
  SERVER. Show a short reveal animation (shaking box, flash, rarity name).
- Price depends on the zone only (the player is not paying for a
  specific rarity):

    Zone | Price (coins)       Zone | Price (coins)
    -----+-------------------  -----+-------------------
     1   |       1K              7  |        1B
     2   |      10K              8  |       10B
     3   |     100K              9  |      100B
     4   |       1M             10  |        1T
     5   |      10M             11  |       10T
     6   |     100M             12  |      100T

  (Price = 1,000 x 10^(zone-1), about 25 kills of sub-zone 2 enemies.)

- Optional features (recommended):
    * Reroll with DIAMONDS: re-roll the rarity of a weapon the player owns
      (keeps the same weapon). Cost: 5 + 2 x zone diamonds. The new rarity
      replaces the old one (the player must confirm).
    * Pity timer: after 30 purchases without an Epic or better, the next
      purchase is guaranteed Epic or better. After 100, guaranteed
      Legendary or better. Reset the counter when it triggers.
    * Salvage: sell unwanted weapons for coins (value = 50% of the zone
      price x rarity tier).
    * Lock: protect a favorite weapon from being salvaged.
- Enemy loot: when an enemy drops a weapon (see enemy_database.txt), it
  is the zone's weapon of that type with the rarity fixed by the loot
  table (not random).

 7. WORKED EXAMPLES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Legendary Iron Shortsword (Plains Sword), used in the Plains:
    10 x 1.0 x 5.5 x 1.40 = 77 damage per hit

Legendary Voidforged Blade (Void Sword), used in the Void:
    1T x 1.0 x 5.5 x 1.40 = 7.7 trillion damage per hit

Same Legendary rarity, but the Void sword is 100 billion times
stronger because of the zone.

Common Lava Hammer (Volcano Heavy), used in the Volcano:
    1M x 2.5 x 1.0 x 1.05 = 2.625M per hit at 0.4 hits/sec
Secret Lava Hammer in the Volcano:
    1M x 2.5 x 25 x 2.20 = 137.5M per hit

Secret weapon from Zone 1 vs a Common weapon from Zone 2:
    Zone 1 Secret Sword in Plains: 10 x 25 x 2.20 = 550
    Zone 2 Common Sword in Desert: 100 x 1.05 = 105
The Zone 1 Secret weapon is still stronger early on, but it is a very
rare item. Once the Desert enemies (800+ HP) are met, the player needs
a Desert weapon for good results. This is the intended progression.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 8. IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
CONFIG MODULES (keep every number in config, never hard-coded)
    Config/WeaponCategories.luau   category multipliers, speed, range, special
    Config/WeaponRarities.luau     multipliers, zone boost, proc chance, odds
    Config/Weapons.luau            96 entries: id, name, category, zone, model
    Config/Zones.luau              zone base damage, element, shop price

INVENTORY ENTRY
    { id = "plains_sword", rarity = 5, level = 0, locked = false }

SERVER RESPONSIBILITIES
    - Roll the rarity on the server with a weighted random function.
    - Validate the purchase (coins, zone unlocked) before rolling.
    - Compute damage with the shared formula; the client may show the
      same numbers but never decides them.
    - Check the attack cooldown and range on the server.
    - Apply the Zone Boost by comparing the player's current zone with
      the weapon's zone.

CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES
    - Shop UI, reveal animation, inventory, equip, "Zone Boost" icon.
    - Attack animations and VFX (VFX plugin, Toolbox, Blender if needed);
      one attack animation set per CATEGORY is enough (8 sets), and each
      weapon model reuses its category animations.
    - Tool models: reuse a mesh per category and change color, size and
      particles per zone. Rarity can add glow, aura and trail.

ASSET PLAN (to avoid making 96 unique models by hand)
    - 8 base animation sets (one per category).
    - 12 zone color/material themes applied to the base meshes.
    - 8 rarity glow/aura levels.
    - Unique custom models only for the Secret rarity or for the first
      weapons the players see.
