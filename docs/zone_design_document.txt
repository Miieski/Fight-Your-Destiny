=====================================================================
 NPC COMBAT GAME - ZONE DESIGN DOCUMENT
 12 zones x 4 sub-zones. Sub-zone 4 is always the boss room.
=====================================================================

---------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. GENERAL RULES FOR EVERY ZONE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STRUCTURE
- Each zone = 4 sub-zones, played in order. The next sub-zone is locked
  behind a gate (door, barrier, bridge, portal) that the player opens by
  paying coins.
- Sub-zone 4 is the boss room: 2 normal enemies, 2 mini-bosses, then the
  boss. The boss spawns only after BOTH mini-bosses are dead.
- After the boss dies, a Rebirth portal/altar appears in the boss room.
- Each zone has its own theme, color palette, lighting, skybox and music,
  so players instantly know where they are.

SUB-ZONE LAYOUT (reuse this template)
- Entrance / safe spot: no enemies, a spawn point, and the gate back.
- Combat area: an open arena where enemies respawn. Wide enough for
  multiple players, with few obstacles so melee combat feels good.
- 3 enemy spawn groups (one per enemy type), spaced apart, so enemy types
  do not mix too much.
- Exit gate: clearly visible and highly readable (glowing, with the price
  shown on a sign above it).
- Landmark: one large recognizable structure to give identity.

SIZE GUIDE (studs)
- Sub-zones 1-3: about 250 x 250 each.
- Boss room: a circular arena, about 150 studs in diameter, with 2
  small side areas where the mini-bosses wait.

DIFFICULTY FEEL (visual)
- Zones get darker, more dramatic and more detailed as the player
  progresses. Early zones are bright and friendly; late zones are
  intimidating.
- Inside a zone, each sub-zone should look slightly more dangerous than
  the previous one.

PERFORMANCE
- Use simple parts and few unions. Use Streaming Enabled.
- Only keep NPCs active in a sub-zone where a player is present.
- Use the same assets with different colors across zones to save time.

BOSS ROOM TEMPLATE
- Circular or square arena with a closed door behind the player.
- Door locks when the fight starts, and unlocks when everyone dies or the
  boss is defeated.
- Two mini-boss alcoves on opposite sides (statues, cages, nests...).
- Boss intro: a small cinematic (camera shake, roar, name banner).
- Health bar at the top of the screen for the boss.
- Reward chest or altar + Rebirth portal appear after the boss is dead.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 1: PLAINS (Minecraft Overworld style)
=====================================================================
Mood: calm, bright, friendly. The tutorial zone.
Palette: fresh greens, sandy yellow, sky blue, stone gray.
Lighting: sunny daytime, soft shadows, light fog in the distance.
Music: relaxed orchestral/acoustic.

Sub-zone 1 - Plains
  Rolling grass hills with a few trees, flowers and small rocks.
  A wooden fence and a small farm house at the entrance. A dirt path
  leads to the gate (a wooden gate with a sign).
  Landmark: windmill on a hill.

Sub-zone 2 - Beach
  Sand, shallow turquoise water, palm trees, rocks and a wrecked boat.
  Wooden docks and crates on the shore. Seagull nests on rocks.
  Landmark: half-sunk pirate ship.
  Gate: a rope bridge or a stone arch between two cliffs.

Sub-zone 3 - Forest
  Dense oak and pine trees, tall grass, mushrooms, fallen logs and
  hunter camps. Light beams (sun rays) between the trees. Spider webs
  between trunks near the exit.
  Landmark: huge ancient tree in the middle.
  Gate: a large mossy stone door that leads into a mountain.

Sub-zone 4 - Cave (BOSS ROOM)
  A mine tunnel with rails, torches, wooden supports and glowing ore.
  The tunnel opens into a huge cavern with stone pillars.
  Mini-boss alcoves: one troll den (bones, a huge club), one spider
  nest with crystals and webs.
  Boss arena: round stone platform with an ancient Golem statue that
  awakens (moss, cracks, glowing runes on its chest).
  Boss: Golem.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 2: DESERT
=====================================================================
Mood: hot, dry, adventurous.
Palette: sand yellow, orange, terracotta red, turquoise water accent.
Lighting: strong sun, warm tint, heat haze, long hard shadows.
Music: percussion, Middle-Eastern flute.

Sub-zone 1 - Dunes
  Big sand dunes with wind ripples, cacti, dead trees and animal skulls.
  Small tents of lost travelers. Light sand particles blowing.
  Landmark: tall rock arch.

Sub-zone 2 - Oasis
  A palm-tree lake in the middle, with an abandoned village around it:
  clay houses, market stalls, clay jars, torn carpets.
  Landmark: stone well and a ruined fountain.

Sub-zone 3 - Canyon
  Tall red cliffs, narrow paths, stone bridges, cave entrances in
  walls, and rock formations. Sandstone ruins on the ledges.
  Landmark: giant stone bridge across the canyon.
  Gate: a huge sandstone door carved with hieroglyphs.

Sub-zone 4 - Pyramid (BOSS ROOM)
  Interior with hieroglyph walls, torches, golden decor, trap tiles
  (visual only) and statues of jackal-headed guards.
  Mini-boss alcoves: Anubis Guard (jackal statue room) and Sandstorm
  Djinn (floating lamp with a swirling sand vortex).
  Boss arena: a golden throne chamber with a big sarcophagus in the
  center that opens.
  Boss: Cursed Pharaoh.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 3: JUNGLE
=====================================================================
Mood: humid, dense, mysterious.
Palette: deep greens, brown, gold accents, bright flower colors.
Lighting: filtered light, green tint, mist near the ground.
Music: tribal drums, birds, insects.

Sub-zone 1 - Jungle Edge
  Thick vegetation, big leaves, vines, wooden totems at the entrance.
  Narrow dirt paths. Many bushes to hide enemies.
  Landmark: giant tree with a tribal sign.

Sub-zone 2 - River
  A winding river with small waterfalls, rope and wooden bridges,
  stepping stones and a floating dock. Lilies and bamboo.
  Landmark: huge waterfall.

Sub-zone 3 - Lost Temple
  Mossy stone ruins covered by roots, broken stairs, carved faces and
  arrow-trap walls (visual). Torches and glowing glyphs.
  Landmark: stepped temple pyramid in the jungle.
  Gate: temple door with a face carving that opens its mouth.

Sub-zone 4 - Temple Heart (BOSS ROOM)
  Inside the temple: golden altar, stone pillars, hanging vines, pools
  of water and floor glyphs that glow on hit.
  Mini-boss alcoves: Elder Jaguar (cave-like den) and Tribal Warlord
  (war camp with banners and skulls).
  Boss arena: circular stone ring with a giant golden idol.
  Boss: Ancestral Gorilla.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 4: TUNDRA
=====================================================================
Mood: cold, harsh, quiet and beautiful.
Palette: white, ice blue, pale purple, dark pine green.
Lighting: pale low sun, blue shadows, snowfall particles, aurora at
  night in the skybox.
Music: wind, soft choir, cold synth.

Sub-zone 1 - Snowy Plains
  Snow fields, snow-covered pine trees, frozen bushes, a broken
  wooden cabin, snowmen scattered around.
  Landmark: lone dead tree with ice hanging from it.

Sub-zone 2 - Frozen Lake
  A huge frozen lake with cracks in the ice, ice-fishing holes and
  small ice caves around the shore. Floating ice chunks.
  Landmark: shipwreck frozen in the ice.

Sub-zone 3 - Mountain
  Steep icy paths, rock walls, snow bridges and ledges, wooden
  scaffolds. Strong wind and snow gusts.
  Landmark: peak with a giant ice cave entrance.
  Gate: an ice gate that melts when opened.

Sub-zone 4 - Ice Citadel (BOSS ROOM)
  Crystal-clear ice walls, blue torches, frozen statues of knights,
  icicles and a frosty floor with sparkles.
  Mini-boss alcoves: Frost Wolf Alpha (ice den with bones) and Ice
  Knight Captain (armory with frozen weapons).
  Boss arena: throne room with a giant ice throne.
  Boss: Frost King.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 5: CURSED SWAMP
=====================================================================
Mood: creepy, wet, magical.
Palette: murky green, dark brown, purple magic, yellow firefly light.
Lighting: low light, thick green fog, glowing plants, fireflies.
Music: croaks, low drones, eerie whispers.

Sub-zone 1 - Marsh
  Shallow stagnant water, reeds, floating lily pads, wooden walkways
  and mud paths. Bubbling pools.
  Landmark: giant half-sunk tree trunk.

Sub-zone 2 - Dead Forest
  Twisted bare trees with faces in the bark, dark mist, glowing
  mushrooms and wisps floating around. Crows on branches.
  Landmark: huge dead tree with an old swing.

Sub-zone 3 - Witch Village
  Crooked cabins on stilts connected by wooden bridges, hanging
  lanterns, potion barrels, scarecrows and cauldrons.
  Landmark: tall crooked witch tower.
  Gate: a large wooden door with a skull knocker.

Sub-zone 4 - Giant Cauldron (BOSS ROOM)
  A big circular pit with a gigantic bubbling cauldron in the center,
  green steam, floating books and potion bottles.
  Mini-boss alcoves: Giant Toad Shaman (toad shrine with totems) and
  Rotting Treant (rotten tree corner with glowing sap).
  Boss arena: ring of stone around the cauldron.
  Boss: Swamp Witch.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 6: VOLCANO
=====================================================================
Mood: hot, aggressive, industrial.
Palette: black rock, orange lava, red glow, ash gray.
Lighting: orange glow, ash particles, red haze, lava light on walls.
Music: deep drums, metallic hits, rumbling.

Sub-zone 1 - Volcano Foot
  Black volcanic rock, ash fields, dead trees, steam vents and small
  lava cracks in the ground. Abandoned miner camp.
  Landmark: giant volcano visible in the background.

Sub-zone 2 - Magma Mine
  Tunnels with rails, minecarts, wooden supports, lava veins in the
  walls and glowing red crystals. Dwarven forges.
  Landmark: huge mining elevator and a big furnace.

Sub-zone 3 - Lava River
  Platforms of basalt over rivers of lava, stone bridges, lava falls
  and floating rocks. Heat distortion.
  Landmark: giant lava waterfall.
  Gate: obsidian door with glowing red cracks.

Sub-zone 4 - Crater (BOSS ROOM)
  Inside the volcano crater: a circular platform surrounded by lava
  with a lava lake below, erupting sparks and falling embers.
  Mini-boss alcoves: Magma Brute (forge with an anvil) and Lava Serpent
  (lava pool nest).
  Boss arena: obsidian ring. A giant stone titan rises from the lava.
  Boss: Magma Titan.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 7: HELL (Minecraft Nether style)
=====================================================================
Mood: oppressive, dramatic, dark.
Palette: red, black, deep orange, purple accents.
Lighting: red sky, dark fog, lava glow, floating embers.
Music: dark choir, heavy bass, screams in the distance.

Sub-zone 1 - Hellish Plains with Lava Lake
  Cracked red-black ground, lava lakes with obsidian islands, bone
  trees and dead plants. Bridges of stone over lava.
  Landmark: giant skull rock in the middle of the lava lake.

Sub-zone 2 - Hell City
  Dark streets, spiked buildings, demon banners, burning braziers,
  cages hanging above and dark arches. Looks like a ruined gothic city.
  Landmark: a tall burning tower in the center.

Sub-zone 3 - Hell Castle Entrance
  Huge gates with demon statues, spikes, a drawbridge over lava,
  guard towers and chains. Fire torches on walls.
  Landmark: giant castle with two demon horns.
  Gate: castle door that opens with chains.

Sub-zone 4 - Hell Throne Room (BOSS ROOM)
  A massive hall with lava rivers on the sides, pillars with faces,
  hanging chains, and a red carpet leading to a giant throne.
  Mini-boss alcoves: Hellhound Alpha (kennel with bones) and Infernal
  Executioner (execution platform with an axe and chains).
  Boss arena: circular platform around the throne.
  Boss: Supreme Demon.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 8: HEAVEN (Minecraft Aether style)
=====================================================================
Mood: majestic, peaceful but powerful. The opposite of Hell.
Palette: white, gold, light blue, soft pink.
Lighting: bright glowing light, god rays, floating light particles.
Music: choir, harps, orchestral swell.

Sub-zone 1 - Sky Island
  Floating grass islands with golden-white trees, waterfalls falling
  into the void, glowing flowers and small floating rocks.
  Landmark: huge floating tree of light.

Sub-zone 2 - Cloud Bridge
  Bridges of clouds and light connecting islands, stone arches,
  floating platforms, wind effects and passing thunder clouds.
  Landmark: giant golden arch in the sky.

Sub-zone 3 - Heaven Castle Entrance
  White marble stairs, golden gates, statues of angels, banners,
  fountains and floating lanterns.
  Landmark: huge castle with golden towers.
  Gate: golden gate opening with light beams.

Sub-zone 4 - Heaven Throne Room (BOSS ROOM)
  Grand white and gold hall with tall columns, stained glass, a
  crystal floor showing the clouds below, and a luminous throne.
  Mini-boss alcoves: Seraph Knight (armory with golden armor) and
  Storm Griffin Lord (nest with storm clouds and lightning).
  Boss arena: circular platform with a halo above.
  Boss: Supreme Angel.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 9: REALM OF THE DEAD
=====================================================================
Mood: haunted, gothic, sad.
Palette: gray, bone white, dark blue, ghostly green.
Lighting: moonlit night, fog, green ghost lights, thunder flashes.
Music: slow organ, whispers, distant bells.

Sub-zone 1 - Graveyard
  Tombstones, crypts, iron fences, dead trees, open graves, crows
  and thick ground fog.
  Landmark: large crypt with a gothic gate.

Sub-zone 2 - Catacombs
  Narrow tunnels full of skulls and bones, candles, stone coffins,
  spiderwebs and tight rooms. Underground, dim and claustrophobic.
  Landmark: huge ossuary room with a bone chandelier.

Sub-zone 3 - Haunted Cathedral
  Ruined gothic cathedral, broken stained glass, floating candles,
  an old organ, collapsed pews and ghostly light beams.
  Landmark: giant cathedral nave with a broken ceiling.
  Gate: a huge sealed door with chains and a skull lock.

Sub-zone 4 - Royal Sarcophagus Room (BOSS ROOM)
  A royal crypt with stone coffins in the walls, a long carpet, tall
  black pillars with green flames and a huge central sarcophagus.
  Mini-boss alcoves: Skeleton General (war banners, armor stands) and
  Banshee Queen (hanging cages and mirrors).
  Boss arena: round platform around the sarcophagus.
  Boss: Lich King.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 10: ABYSS (ocean)
=====================================================================
Mood: beautiful at first, then terrifying and deep.
Palette: turquoise, deep blue, black, neon accents (cyan, pink).
Lighting: underwater caustics, fog and bubbles, fading light with
  depth, glowing creatures.
Music: muffled, deep, atmospheric, sonar pings.

Sub-zone 1 - Coral Reef
  Colorful coral, sea plants, fish swarms, sand floor and bright
  light rays from the surface.
  Landmark: giant coral arch.

Sub-zone 2 - Shipwreck
  Broken ships, chests, anchors, ropes, barrels and rusted cannons.
  Darker water and seaweed.
  Landmark: giant sunken galleon.

Sub-zone 3 - Abyssal Trench
  A deep dark canyon with glowing creatures, bioluminescent plants,
  strong current effects and giant bones on the floor.
  Landmark: giant skeleton of a sea monster.
  Gate: giant clam door with a glowing pearl.

Sub-zone 4 - Sunken Palace (BOSS ROOM)
  An ancient Atlantis-like palace with golden domes, coral-covered
  columns, tridents and a large circular hall.
  Mini-boss alcoves: Kraken Spawn (tentacle-filled cave) and Triton
  Captain (guard post with a trident rack).
  Boss arena: round platform; the Leviathan emerges from the dark
  ocean around the palace.
  Boss: Leviathan.
  Tip: if swimming mechanics are too complex, make the zone "air
  bubbles / dry" (an underwater dome look) with walking gameplay.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 11: MECHANICAL CITY
=====================================================================
Mood: cold, industrial, sci-fi.
Palette: steel gray, neon cyan and orange, black, warning yellow.
Lighting: artificial lights, neon strips, sparks, steam, red alarms.
Music: electronic, industrial beats.

Sub-zone 1 - Abandoned Factory
  Conveyor belts, giant gears, pipes, crates, broken machines and
  hanging cables. Rusty metal floors and dim yellow lights.
  Landmark: giant assembly line machine.

Sub-zone 2 - Industrial Sewers
  Large pipes, toxic green sludge channels, steam vents, metal
  walkways and valves.
  Landmark: giant pipe junction with a waterfall of sludge.

Sub-zone 3 - Control Center
  Screens on the walls, laser grids, server racks, glass panels and
  security gates. Blue/cyan lights.
  Landmark: big central hologram map.
  Gate: security door with a keypad and a red laser.

Sub-zone 4 - Reactor Core (BOSS ROOM)
  A huge round reactor chamber with a glowing core in the center,
  metal catwalks, energy pillars and warning signs.
  Mini-boss alcoves: War Mech (hangar with a weapons rack) and
  Sentinel Prime (charging station with energy tubes).
  Boss arena: metal platform around the reactor.
  Boss: Colossal Robot.

=====================================================================
 ZONE 12: THE VOID (Minecraft End style)
=====================================================================
Mood: final, mysterious, epic.
Palette: black, deep purple, magenta glow, pale cream/gray stone.
Lighting: dark starfield skybox, purple glow, floating particles.
Music: slow, huge, ambient, with a distant choir.

Sub-zone 1 - Void Island
  Floating islands of obsidian and pale stone, surrounded by endless
  void. Tall obsidian pillars. Purple particles rising.
  Landmark: giant floating pillar with a crystal on top.

Sub-zone 2 - Crystal Forest
  Trees made of purple crystal, dark ground, glowing plants and
  floating crystal shards. Soft chiming sounds.
  Landmark: giant crystal tree.

Sub-zone 3 - Voyagers' City
  Ruins of an ancient city: broken towers, floating bridges, stairs
  that lead nowhere, statues and lanterns of purple light.
  Landmark: huge broken tower floating in the void.
  Gate: a portal ring that activates with a rift effect.

Sub-zone 4 - Heart of the Void (BOSS ROOM)
  A circular arena with obsidian pillars topped by crystals, a ring
  of floating stones and a swirling void in the sky.
  Mini-boss alcoves: Void Reaper (dark altar with a scythe) and
  Crystal Warden (crystal cage with runes).
  Boss arena: wide platform with a central dragon altar.
  Boss: Void Dragon (final boss).

=====================================================================
 TRANSITIONS & HUB (suggestions)
=====================================================================
- A central HUB (spawn): shop, weapon seller, diamond/Robux shop,
  rebirth altar, pets/eggs, and a portal to each unlocked zone.
- Zone portals appear in the hub as large themed doorways; locked
  zones are grayed out and show the required rebirth count.
- Each zone's first sub-zone has a return-to-hub teleporter in the
  safe spot.
- Every boss room has a one-way entrance until the fight ends.

=====================================================================
 BUILD ORDER REMINDER
=====================================================================
Build and test 1 full zone (Plains) first. Then reuse the same
sub-zone template, gates, spawners and boss room for the other zones,
changing only the visuals, enemies and config values.
