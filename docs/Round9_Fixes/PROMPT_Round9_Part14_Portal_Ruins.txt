PART 14 - PORTAL RUINS: SHOW WHERE THE NEXT PORTAL IS (image 22)
Because locked portals are now invisible (PART 12), the player must still see where the NEXT portal will be. Marker chosen by the owner: RUINS OF THE PORTAL. Image 22 shows the current Plains portal (stone brick arch with a name plaque and green gems); use it as the style reference for the bricks of the ruins.
VISIBILITY (personal, client-side, like the other unlock states)
- A player sees the ruins ONLY for the NEXT portal to unlock (the first locked zone after the ones they already unlocked). All later locked portals stay completely invisible. When a portal is unlocked, the next one's ruins appear (with a small soft fade-in). After a Rebirth the "next" portal follows the new unlock state (the ruins of the new next portal appear, unlocked portals stay built). Server keeps refusing teleports; handle StreamingEnabled (use Config coordinates/anchors so the marker can be built when streamed in), late joiners and several players seeing different states.
THE RUINS (each zone in its own portal style/colors, built from the SAME brick pieces as that zone's real portal so the build animation can reuse them)
- Two broken pillar bases (2-4 courses of bricks at different heights, chipped tops), a cracked foundation slab with a worn faint rune circle (very dim glow in the zone color), a few arch fragments and loose bricks lying around in a small pile, moss/ivy/weeds (jungle: roots; desert: sand drifts; tundra: snow and ice; swamp: slime and dead roots), small details (cracks, pebbles). Same low-mid poly, beveled, textured like the improved portals. Footprint roughly the footprint of the real portal; low enough that it never blocks a path or a player (no tall collision, only the pillar bases collide, nothing the player can get stuck on).
- A small wooden or stone SIGN beside it (in the new wood UI style): zone name, and the requirements: "Boss key: owned / missing", "Gold: price" (green if the player can afford it, red if not) and a button/ProximityPrompt "Open Zones menu" (or Buy directly if the PART 12 purchase flow allows it). No "???" anywhere. Texts and prices come from Config/RebirthZoneUnlock (the rules already implemented) and update live.
UNLOCK ANIMATION (replaces the "from nothing" start of PART 12)
- When the zone is unlocked for the player, the portal is built FROM the ruins: the loose bricks and the scattered fragments rise and fly back to their places, the broken pillar stumps grow back course by course, the arch closes from the sides toward the keystone, moss and cracks fade away, the sign slides into the ground, then the portal activates (gems/runes/field light up with the emissive ramp, particles, sound), as already specified (3-5 s, dust puffs, stone sounds, client side, once). A player joining with the zone unlocked sees the finished lit portal instantly.
GUIDANCE ("Locate" button, decided by the owner)
- In the Zones menu, the next zone shows a "Locate" button: it draws a temporary glowing guide (a line/beam of small lights or a floating arrow path) from the player to the ruins for about 10 seconds with the distance in meters, then fades. It works from any zone and from the hub (use stored Config positions, not loaded instances), is cheap for mobile (a few Beams/Parts, no per-frame heavy work) and cancels cleanly if the player teleports or the portal is unlocked.
CONFIG AND DOCS
- Config/PortalRuins.luau (per-zone ruin template ids, colors, sign text format, locate settings). Register the ruins in the asset preview (one per zone, in each zone block, medium row) and in docs/prop_inventory.md and docs/asset_preview_index.md/.json. MapProps/SyncMapProps rules apply.
TESTS AND PHOTOS

- Play mode: new player (only the first ruins visible), unlock then next ruins appear, rejoin, Rebirth, two players with different states, Locate from the hub and from a far zone, phone emulator, no Output errors.
